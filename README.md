# The Weight of Stars — David Amoateng

**A true story · Wassa Mampong, Ghana → Moscow, Russia**

*The Weight of Stars* is David Amoateng’s personal documentary and academic website. It brings together the story of his journey from a farming community in Wassa Mampong, Ghana, through school, university, work, and migration, to completing a Master’s in Population and Development at HSE University in Moscow with distinction. It also presents his research, publications, professional experience, academic interests, and the people and memories that shaped the journey.

The website is designed as a long-form personal documentary rather than a conventional résumé page. Its visual identity combines midnight navy, ivory, and champagne gold, with cinematic photography, chapter-based storytelling, responsive galleries, and selected video memories.

## What the website contains

- **The story:** six chapters tracing childhood and farm life in Wassa Mampong, the years at Simms Senior High School, the University of Ghana and Commonwealth Hall, national service, the long wait for a professional opportunity, Zeroman’s call, and life and study in Moscow.
- **The journey statement:** a centred, bold line connecting the classroom of First Star Academy in Wassa Mampong to the corners of Simms Senior High School and the milestones that followed.
- **Family and roots:** photographs and memories of David’s father, Op. Kojo Amoateng, and mother, Adwoa Ntiakowaa, alongside family and generational memories.
- **Education and achievement:** academic milestones, including First Class Honours at the University of Ghana and the HSE University master’s graduation with a Red Diploma/distinction.
- **HSE memories:** graduation portraits, the ceremony, friends and classmates, the graduation gathering, the university building, and the group graduation video. The gallery also includes the people David describes as part of his Moscow family and support network.
- **Research and academic profile:** research interests, thesis work on artisanal and small-scale gold mining and agricultural livelihoods in Wassa-Amenfi East, Ghana, research projects, publications, and scholarly work.
- **Professional experience:** selected employment, teaching, internship, and national-service experience.
- **People and acknowledgements:** friends, mentors, classmates, colleagues, and family members who contributed to different stages of the journey.
- **Media gallery:** photographs and video clips from childhood and farm life, Simms, Legon and Commonwealth Hall, PRESEC national service, and Moscow.
- **CV and contact:** a downloadable CV and links to David’s professional and research profiles.

## Technology and design

This is a static website built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It does not require a database, server-side application, package installation, or build process. The CSS provides the visual design and responsive layouts, while the JavaScript supports lightweight interface behaviour. Some externally hosted typography may require an internet connection. Local photographs, videos, and the CV are served from the `assets` folder.

The site is designed to work on desktop, tablet, and mobile screens. Horizontal photo galleries can be explored by swiping or scrolling where supported, and the visual presentation uses the navy, ivory, and champagne-gold palette established for the documentary.

## Content and editorial approach

The site is built around a first-person account of David’s life. The narrative follows the chronology of his education and work while retaining the personal and emotional context of family, friendship, perseverance, migration, and academic achievement.

The site includes personal photographs of family and friends. These images are presented as memories from David’s life and are not intended to imply professional affiliations or roles that are not stated in the accompanying text.
